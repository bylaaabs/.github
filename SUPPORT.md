# support

how to get help with something by laaabs.

- **github discussions** - [github.com/orgs/bylaaabs/discussions](https://github.com/orgs/bylaaabs/discussions) for questions, ideas and help from us and from other people. the first place to look: it may already be answered.
- **email** - `support@laaabs.com` for anything private: an account, a payment, a licence, a device.
- **the contact page** - [laaabs.com/contact](https://laaabs.com/contact) if you prefer a form.

found a bug? open an issue on the product's repo with the bug form. found a vulnerability? never in public: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

we are three people, so an answer can take a few days. status of the services is at [status.laaabs.com](https://status.laaabs.com).
